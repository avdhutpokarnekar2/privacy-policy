1. Information We Collect
We only collect personal data that is necessary to deliver and improve our services:

Account & Profile Data: Name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and login credentials when you register.

Order & Transaction Data: Order history, items purchased, delivery preferences, and payment verification IDs (payment details like UPI or cards are handled securely by third-party payment gateways; we do not store full card details).

Technical & Usage Data: Device IP address, operating system, unique device identifiers, app usage statistics, and log files.

Location Data: Coarse or precise geolocation data (with your consent) to calculate delivery fees, find nearby outlets, and assign delivery partners.

2. How We Use Your Information
We process your personal data based on your consent and for specified, lawful purposes, including:

Service Delivery: To fulfill food/retail orders, manage deliveries, send order status updates, and process payments.

Account Management: To create, maintain, and secure your user account.

Customer Support: To respond to your queries, issues, or refund requests.

App Improvement: To monitor server performance, diagnose bugs, and improve user experience.

Legal & Security Obligations: To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, unauthorized activity, or legal compliance demands.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted third-party service providers (Data Processors) bound by confidentiality agreements:

Delivery Partners: Sharing delivery address and recipient contact details to complete fulfillments.

Payment Gateways: Redirecting payment requests to authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay, Cashfree, Paytm).

Cloud Infrastructure Services: Hosting our server and database securely on cloud providers (e.g., Amazon Web Services / Google Cloud Platform).

Legal Authorities: Disclosing information if mandated by law enforcement or regulatory authorities under statutory obligations.

4. Data Security & Storage
Security Safeguards: We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL), access controls, and firewall configurations to safeguard your data against loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

Data Retention: We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal, tax, or accounting requirements. Once data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights (Data Principal Rights)
Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access: Request information about what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.

Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updates to inaccurate data or request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for processing at any time through account settings or by contacting us.

Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit complaints regarding data processing directly to our Grievance Officer.

6. Children's Privacy
Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. We will notify you of any material updates by posting the revised policy inside the app/website along with an updated "Last Updated" date.
