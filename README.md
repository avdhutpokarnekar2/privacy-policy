Privacy Policy for City Chicken

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to City Chicken . We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use the City Chicken mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect
We collect personal data that is necessary to deliver and improve our services:

Account & Profile Data: Name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and login credentials created when you register.

Order & Transaction Data: Order history, items purchased, delivery preferences, and payment verification photo.

Technical & Usage Data: Device IP address, operating system, unique device identifiers, app usage statistics, and log files.


2. How We Use Your Information
We process your personal data based on your consent and for lawful purposes, including:

Service Delivery: To fulfill food and retail orders, manage deliveries, send order status updates, and process payments.

Account Management: To create, maintain, and secure your user account.

Customer Support: To respond to your queries, support issues, or refund requests.

App Improvement: To monitor server performance, diagnose bugs, and improve overall user experience.

Legal & Security Obligations: To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, unauthorized activity, or legal compliance demands.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell your personal information.

Delivery Partners: Sharing your delivery address and contact details to complete order fulfillments.

Cloud Infrastructure: Hosting our servers and databases securely on cloud providers AWS.

Legal Authorities: Disclosing information if mandated by law enforcement or regulatory authorities under statutory obligations.

4. Data Security & Retention
Security Safeguards: We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL), access controls, and firewall configurations to safeguard your data against loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.

Data Retention: We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Once no longer required, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights (Data Principal Rights)
Under applicable data protection laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have the following rights:

Right to Access: Request information about what personal data we process and how it is used.

Right to Correction & Erasure: Request updates to inaccurate data or ask for the deletion of your account and personal information.

Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time via your app account settings or by contacting us.

Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit complaints regarding data processing directly to our Grievance Officer.

6. Children's Privacy
Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. We will notify you of material updates by posting the revised policy inside the app or website with an updated "Last Updated" date.
